## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a dignified, transparent, and grounded digital civic platform tailored for rural local self-governance across Maharashtra. Rooted in the agricultural heritage of the Deccan plateau, the visual identity departs from congested, bureaucratic government portals in favor of warm, editorial public architecture.

The personality balances civic authority with approachable communal trust:
- **Tone:** Dignified, transparent, rooted, and high-clarity.
- **Audience:** Dual-audience ecosystem comprising multi-generational rural citizens (requiring high contrast, large touch targets, and bilingual Devanagari/English parity) and Gram Panchayat administrative functionaries managing multi-village registries, schemes, tax records, and certificates.
- **Visual Style:** Modern Editorial Folk-Civic. It merges clean structural lines, spacious warm surfaces, organic earth tones (deep forest green, dry terracotta, soothing parchment cream), and low-contrast borders instead of noisy drop-shadows. The result evokes official legitimacy without sterile bureaucracy.

## Colors

The color architecture is derived from the fertile landscapes and cultural materiality of rural Maharashtra:

- **Primary (`#134E39`):** Deep Forest Green. Anchors official navigation headers, key action buttons, and sovereign civic signifiers. Evokes agriculture, prosperity, and communal governance.
- **Secondary (`#D96B27`):** Warm Terracotta / Saffron. Deployed purposefully for primary calls-to-action, urgent public notices, and status indicators. Provides warm cultural visibility without aggressive saturation.
- **Tertiary (`#82B93C`):** Fresh Harvest Green. Used for positive verification tags, successful application progress meters, and ecological development initiatives.
- **Neutral (`#212529` & `#1E2621`):** Deep Charcoal with an organic green-black undertone. Preserves pristine legibility across high-ambient sunlight conditions on mobile screens.
- **Backgrounds (`#FBF9F5` & `#F4EFE6`):** Replaces harsh sterile screens with warm, natural stone and parchment textures that reduce eye fatigue for field workers and rural users.
- **Accent Gold (`#F5D77F`):** Derived from brass artefacts and celebratory saffron-turmeric tones, applied to badges of distinction, Gram Sabha announcements, and key civic milestones.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes uncompromising bilingual clarity. Noto Sans serves as the universal workhorse, ensuring native glyph support for Devanagari (Marathi) alongside Latin script with optical harmony and identical x-height metrics.

- **Devanagari Balance:** Line heights are calibrated 8–12% taller than Latin-only standards to account for the upper matras (shirorēkhā vowel modifiers) and lower descenders without vertical clipping.
- **Legibility under Sunlight:** Font weights lean substantial; regular body copy starts at weight `400` with generous line-heights, while headlines utilize weight `600` and `700` to prevent letter degradation on budget smartphone displays.
- **Numbers and Official Data:** All numerical figures (survey numbers, citizen IDs, revenue metrics) use proportional lining figures for clean tabular alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system for administrative and desktop portals, collapsing to a sturdy 4-column structure on mobile devices:

- **Touch Ergonomics:** Spacing tokens reflect physical outdoor realities. Action targets respect a strict minimum interactive dimension of 48px × 48px, preventing mis-taps on mobile devices in transit or field conditions.
- **Multi-Village Hierarchy:** Standard pages feature a two-tiered header: a persistent civic jurisdiction bar (Village / Taluka / District selector) followed by the functional application navigation.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked cards, sticky bottom action bar for primary actions, full-width form inputs, edge margins of `1rem`.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with 2-column citizen service grids and collapsible administrative side drawers.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout (max-width `1280px`) flanked by natural margins (`2.5rem`) to prevent broad horizontal scanning fatigue during intensive data entry.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects heavy drop shadows, translucent blurs, and neon glows, which read as commercial or artificial in rural governance contexts. Instead, it employs **Tonal Layering combined with Low-Contrast Borders**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** `surface-cream` (`#FBF9F5`) as the universal warm backdrop.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` containers resting directly on the cream base, demarcated by a 1px border of `border-subtle` (`#E5DEC9`).
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Modals):** Uses a single subtle, ambient diffused shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 38, 33, 0.08)`, paired with the crisp `#E5DEC9` outline.
- **Active / Pressed Elevation:** Instead of physical extrusion, actionable components indicate state changes through deliberate color tone shifts (e.g., `#134E39` shifting to deep pine `#0E3E2C`) and inner stroke emphasis.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** shape language:
- **Base Components:** Radius set to `0.25rem` (4px) for input fields, table cells, and compact action controls, communicating administrative precision, structure, and order.
- **Container Cards & Dialogues (`rounded-lg`):** Set to `0.5rem` (8px), gently softening large informational blocks without losing structural geometry.
- **Pills and Badges:** Status tokens and category filters use fully rounded pill profiles (`9999px`) to create immediate visual distinction from utilitarian cards and buttons.
- **Cultural Identity Motif:** Decorative headers and scheme announcement frames may utilize subtle asymmetric top-right notched corners (6px cut or step) paying quiet homage to traditional stone pillar architecture without compromising digital cleanliness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Civic Action:** Deep Forest Green (`#134E39`) background, white text, 48px height, 4px border radius. Hover transitions to `#0E3E2C`.
- **Secondary / Focal Action:** Warm Terracotta (`#D96B27`) background, white text. Reserved for high-priority citizen tasks: "Pay Property Tax", "File Grievance", "Download Birth Certificate".
- **Outline Button:** Transparent background, 1.5px border of `#134E39`, text `#134E39`. Used for secondary workflows and report downloads.
- **Disabled State:** Background `#E5DEC9`, text `#8C978F`, non-interactive cursor.

### Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** 48px minimum height. Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E5DEC9`, text `#1E2621`.
- **Focus State:** 2px border `#134E39` with an outer ring of `rgba(19, 78, 57, 0.15)`. No vague browser outlines.
- **Labels:** Bilingual stacked format where necessary (e.g., "पूर्ण नाव / Full Name") using `label-md` in `#1E2621` positioned consistently above the field.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Official Notice:** Background `#F5D77F` with `#1E2621` text.
- **Scheme Approved / Valid:** Background `#E8F5E9` with `#1E7E34` border and text.
- **Pending Review:** Background `#FFF3E0` with `#D96B27` border and text.
- **Shape:** Compact pill (`rounded-full`) with 4px vertical and 10px horizontal padding.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Citizen Service Card:** Clean `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px `#E5DEC9` outline. Displays an official iconography tile, Marathi service name (`title-lg`), English subtitle (`body-sm`), and a right-aligned action arrow.
- **Notice Board Card:** Cream tint (`#F4EFE6`) border-left accent of 4px solid `#D96B27` for Gram Sabha notifications and emergency agricultural advisories.

### Village Switcher & Administrative Header
- **Context Banner:** Top-level persistent strip in `#0E3E2C` featuring the national and state insignia placeholders, the active Gram Panchayat name (e.g., "ग्रामपंचायत कोमलवाडी / GP Komalwadi"), and quick toggle for Taluka/Village administrative scope.
